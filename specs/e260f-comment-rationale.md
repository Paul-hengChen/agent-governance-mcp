## test/e120-void-recut-refusal.test.mjs

Section 1, the nine-indent matrix, was raised in review round 2 as finding R2-C1 and is the highest-value property: it caught two successive incomplete fixes (round 1's `\b` boundary bug, round 2's `[ \t]*` subset of `trim()`).
