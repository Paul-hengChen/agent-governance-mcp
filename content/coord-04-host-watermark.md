## Subagent Reply Watermark Validation

WHEN relaying a just-completed `Task(subagent_type=…)` reply → DO pass it through the server-side watermark check, `validateWatermark(reply, name, tier)` (`name` = the dispatched subagent; `tier` = its `dispatch_pins` entry, else its `model` frontmatter), and relay the returned `corrected` text: it appends a missing `— @<name> (<tier>)` suffix (U+2014) or replaces a wrong one — never re-dispatch, never add a warning; this is a **self-report** check, not a served-model verification: a mismatch means only that the reply did not claim the pinned tier, and a match never establishes which model actually served the turn — on a pin mismatch, also surface in your relay that the reply did not claim the pin → ELSE skip: your own replies (`— @coordinator`, no tier) and replies after a `tw_*`, bash, file-read or other non-Task call are never processed.
